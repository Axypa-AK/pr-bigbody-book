The Big Body
Ben was the biggest kid in his class. He was tall, strong, and had broad shoulders. Some people thought he looked scary, but Ben was actually kind and gentle.

One rainy afternoon, Ben saw a little boy crying near the school gate. The boy had dropped his books in a puddle.

Ben quickly picked them up and used his big jacket to keep the boy dry. Then he carried the heavy books all the way to the boy’s house.

“Thank you!” the boy said with a smile.

Ben smiled back. He realized that having a big body wasn’t just about being strong—it meant he could use his strength to help others.

chapter 2

The big Body

Ben was the biggest kid in his class. He was tall, strong, and had broad shoulders. Some people thought he looked scary, but Ben was actually kind and gentle.

One rainy afternoon, Ben was walking home from school when he heard someone crying near the school gate. A little boy had dropped his books in a puddle. The pages were wet, and his backpack was too heavy for him to lift.

“Don’t worry,” Ben said kindly. “I’ll help you.”

He picked up the books one by one and carefully brushed off the mud. Then he took off his big jacket and held it over the boy’s head to protect him from the rain.

“Thank you,” the little boy said. “I thought I would have to walk home with all my books in the rain.”

“What’s your name?” Ben asked.

“I’m Leo. I’m in the first grade.”

Ben smiled. “I’m Ben. Let’s walk together. Where do you live?”

They started walking down the street. Ben carried Leo’s heavy backpack, while Leo held the books close to his chest. On the way, they saw an old woman trying to move a large box into her shop.

“Would you like some help?” Ben asked.

The woman nodded. Ben carefully lifted the box and carried it inside. The woman thanked him and gave the boys two warm cookies.

When they finally reached Leo’s house, Leo’s mother opened the door. She was worried because Leo was late, but she smiled when she saw Ben helping him.

“Thank you for taking care of my son,” she said.

Ben felt happy. People often noticed his big body, but that day he understood something important: being strong was not about making others afraid. It was about using his strength to help people.



From then on, Ben became known not as the biggest kid in school, but as one of the kindest.
